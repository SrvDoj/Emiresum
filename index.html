Projects

Telegram Student Council Bot

What it does

A Telegram bot developed for my school's student council that helps students explore topics related to different school subjects. A student opens the bot and chooses a grade from 7 to 11, then a subject. The bot provides the textbooks for that subject, and students can choose the pages they need and open a solutions book (the teacher's book with answers).

The problem it helps solve

Students can find the books they need for each subject in one place instead of searching for them separately.

My contribution

The bot was made by the student council team that I led as Minister of Education.
